INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective as of June 1, 2026, by and between Lumen Analytics LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Company"), and Priya Nair, an individual ("Contractor").

SERVICES AND SCOPE

1.1 Services. Contractor shall deliver the reporting dashboard described in Exhibit A. Any change to the scope requires a written change order signed by both parties before the work starts.

COMPENSATION

2.2 Payment Due. Invoices are payable within fifteen (15) days after delivery of the deliverables.

2.3 Taxes. All fees are inclusive of all applicable taxes.

4.3 Limitation of Liability. In no event shall Contractor's aggregate liability under this Agreement exceed the total fees paid to Contractor in the preceding twelve months.

TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Term. This Agreement begins on June 1, 2026 and continues for a term of twelve (12) months.

5.2 Expiration. This Agreement shall expire on December 31, 2027 and shall not renew.

5.3 Automatic Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive terms of twelve (12) months each unless either party gives notice of non-renewal.

5.4 Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause on thirty (30) days written notice.

5.5 Termination for Cause. If Company terminates this Agreement for breach, Contractor shall not be entitled to any payment for work performed.

5.6 Payment on Termination. Upon any termination Company shall pay Contractor for all services performed through the termination date.
