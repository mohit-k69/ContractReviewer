INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective as of October 1, 2025, by and between Northwind Digital LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Company"), and Jordan Rivera, an individual residing in Austin, Texas ("Contractor").

SERVICES AND SCOPE OF WORK

1.1 Services. Contractor shall provide web development and design services and such additional services as may be requested by Company from time to time, including unlimited revisions until Company is satisfied.

COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT

2.1 Fees. Company shall pay Contractor at the rate of $60.00 per hour for approved hours worked.

2.2 Payment Due. Invoices are payable Net 75 days from receipt of a valid invoice. Payment is conditioned on Company being paid by its own client for the related work.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

3.1 Ownership. Contractor hereby assigns to Company all work product, inventions, and intellectual property created by Contractor, whether or not related to the Services and including any pre-existing tools or materials, immediately upon creation.

CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Confidentiality. Contractor shall keep confidential all information disclosed by Company, whether or not marked confidential. These obligations shall continue in perpetuity.

TERMINATION

5.1 Termination for Convenience. Company may terminate this Agreement at any time without notice. Contractor may not terminate this Agreement before completion of the Services.

RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

6.1 Non-Competition. During the term and for a period of twenty-four (24) months after termination, Contractor shall not directly or indirectly provide services to any competing business anywhere in the United States.

6.2 Exclusivity. Contractor shall provide services exclusively to Company during the term.

LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

7.1 Indemnification. Contractor shall indemnify and hold harmless Company from any and all claims, losses, damages and legal fees arising out of or in connection with the Services.

DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration in New York, New York.
